The palette and system-serif typography are copied byte-for-byte at build time
from tgui/prototypes/ui-shared/tokens.css, originally seeded from the crafting
workbench. Existing studies and production code are not copied or modified.
There are no bundled third-party runtime dependencies, fonts, images or scripts.
The source/ directory preserves every exact input needed to reproduce this hub,
including the shared token source and repository license. manifest.json records
source and output SHA-256 hashes. Preserve these sources, instructions, notices
and license when distributing the hub. Linked studies are separate artifacts;
their corresponding-source and licensing obligations remain separate and must
also be preserved by the host. This build does not publish or deploy anything.
